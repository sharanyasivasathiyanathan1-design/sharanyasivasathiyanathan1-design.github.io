Scotiabank · Canada's Top Students Conference 2025 · November 8, 2025

ScotiaOne's Strategy to Engage Gen Z

Team: Sharanya Sivasathiyanathan, Adnan Maghari, Leeza Grishpul, Nadia Zhang, Alex Zhang

Tasked with bridging the gap between Scotiabank and Gen Z banking behaviours, our team proposed "Scoti the Dog" — a three-pillar digital strategy built on personalized financial tools, gamified financial literacy, and an AI-powered advisory chatbot. The recommendation addressed core barriers of trust, accessibility, and security, backed by a decision matrix and five-year implementation roadmap.

Pillar 1 — Tools For You: Personalized budget tracking, credit monitoring, savings goals
Pillar 2 — Scoti's School: Gamified financial literacy linked to Scene+ rewards
Pillar 3 — Ask Scoti: AI chatbot enhancing Smart Money Advice+ for personalized guidance

FreshSight Queen's Consulting · Case Competition 2026

The People Behind the Progress: Rethinking Recruitment at the Canadian Cancer Society

Team: Sharanya Sivasathiyanathan, Prerana Sil, Warner Schaettgen, Tanry Wang, Jingyu Zhao, Prisha Bhatia

The Canadian Cancer Society sought to scale Relay For Life Youth (RFLY) across high schools while reducing organizer burden. Our winning recommendation embedded RFLY as a culminating module within YRDSB Business Leadership courses, turning extracurricular reliance into a standardized, board-level curriculum model with repeatable year-over-year participation and a clear path to province-wide expansion.

Problem: Teacher burnout and opt-in school adoption limiting RFLY scalability
Solution: BOF4M curriculum integration pilot across 33 YRDSB schools
Impact: Projected $20M/year revenue growth by 2028 with board-level mandate
