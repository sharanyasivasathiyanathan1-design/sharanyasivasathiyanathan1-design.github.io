About Me

I'm a first-year Commerce student at Smith School of Business, Queen's University, and one of 50 recipients of the Chancellor's Scholarship. My work spans finance, governance, and social policy — I believe markets work better when they work for everyone.

As CEO of the Ontario Student Trustees' Association, I help shape education policy and lead province-wide conferences. As an Investment Analyst with Queen's Sustainable Business Initiative, I apply an ESG lens to research that matters.

I've spoken on CBC News Radio, published in the Queen's Business Review, organized rallies on Parliament Hill, and was selected for the City of Ottawa's firefighter training program. I care deeply about access — to opportunities, information, and community.
