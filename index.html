About Me

I am a first-year Commerce student at Smith School of Business, Queen's University, and one of 50 recipients of the Chancellor's Scholarship. My interests sit at the intersection of human capital management, consulting, and public policy. I believe organizations work better when their people do.

My current research, funded through the SSHRC Undergraduate Student Summer Research Fellowship (USSRF) in Organizational Behaviour, examines how unstructured, project-based work environments amplify gender and racial bias in performance evaluation and agentic decision-making. I design and analyze survey instruments in Qualtrics and conduct literature reviews drawing on organizational psychology and labour economics.

Outside the lab, I write on financial markets and consumer policy for the Queen's Business Review, compete in consulting case competitions, and serve as COO of the Ontario Student Trustees' Association, where I lead conference planning and governance for a province-wide student leadership body.
